The Big Animal Party 🎉🐯🦒🐘

In a sunny jungle, Elephant planned a huge party! He invited Tiger, Giraffe, Monkey, and all his friends.

Monkey decorated with bananas, Giraffe hung colorful leaves high up, and Tiger played fun games.

Everyone danced, sang, and shared yummy fruits. It was the best party ever!

Moral: Sharing and celebrating with friends makes everyone happy! 🥳
